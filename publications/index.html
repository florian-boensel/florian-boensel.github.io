  1. arXiv
    Atom-Photon Bound States in Fractal Photonic Lattices: Localization Length and Anomalous Diffusion
    Florian Bönsel, Flore K. Kunst, and Federico Roccati
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.23625, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib

    We study atom-photon bound states seeded by two-level emitters coupled to self-similar (fractal) photonic lattices. We show that the far-field localization length obeys a scaling law governed by anomalous diffusion, without relying on translational invariance or a band-edge effective-mass approximation. Exact diagonalization on Sierpiński gaskets, pyramids, Vicsek graphs, and Sierpiński carpets confirms the predictions; in the near field, the bound-state amplitude exhibits an additional algebraic variation.

  2. arXiv
    A Helmholtz Equation for Surface Plasmon Polaritons on Curved Interfaces: Controlling Cooperativity with Geometric Potentials
    Florian Bönsel and Flore K. Kunst
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2603.27702, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib

    We derive a covariant, effective two-dimensional wave equation for the transverse-magnetic surface plasmon polariton mode on weakly curved smooth metal-dielectric interfaces. A perturbative expansion of Maxwell’s equations with curvature-adapted boundary conditions yields a Helmholtz equation with two geometric potential terms that are linear in the extrinsic curvature and distinguish convex from concave interfaces. As an application, we demonstrate sign-dependent cooperative frequency shifts and a curvature-driven redistribution of superradiant and subradiant decay rates for a ring of quantum emitters on a curved metallic spheroid.

  3. Quantum
    Fibonacci Waveguide Quantum Electrodynamics
    Florian Bönsel, Flore K. Kunst, and Federico Roccati
    Quantum, 2026
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib

    We investigate waveguide quantum electrodynamics in a one-dimensional photonic array whose hopping strengths are structured aperiodically according to the deterministic Fibonacci-Lucas substitution rule. These "Fibonacci waveguides" lack translational invariance and feature a singular continuous spectrum and critical eigenstates, representing a deterministic intermediate between order and disorder. We show how to achieve decoherence-free, coherent interactions in this setting, establishing aperiodic waveguides as a versatile and experimentally feasible platform for engineering quantum emitter interactions.

  1. Phys. Rev. A
    Idealized Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen States from Non–Phase-Matched Parametric Down-Conversion
    C. Okoth, E. Kovlakov, Florian Bönsel, and 4 more authors
    Physical Review A, 2020
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib

    We show that reducing a highly nonlinear material to micrometer dimensions relaxes longitudinal phase-matching constraints. Using a micron-sized lithium niobate layer, we achieve record levels of transverse-wavevector entanglement, with an estimated 1200+ entangled angular modes, confirmed through correlation measurements and stimulated emission tomography. Such highly entangled photonic states can enhance quantum information capacity and improve resolution in quantum imaging.
