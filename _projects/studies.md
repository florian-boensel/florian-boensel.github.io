During my studies at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg I worked on several research projects across
different fields:

  quadratic inflaton field in loop quantum cosmology.
- **Biophysics** — *Group of Prof. A.-S. Smith* — simulation of 2D tissue growth on a
  substrate.
