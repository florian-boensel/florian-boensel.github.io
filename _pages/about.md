I am a doctoral researcher in the [Kunst Research Group](https://mpl.mpg.de/research-at-mpl/independent-research-groups/kunst-research-group) at the **Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light** in Erlangen, Germany, where I am a member of the International Max Planck Research School (IMPRS-PL).

My research lies at the intersection of **theoretical photonics, plasmonics, and waveguide quantum electrodynamics**. I am particularly interested in how *geometry* and *aperiodic order* shape light–matter interaction — from surface plasmon polaritons on curved metal interfaces to atom–photon bound states in fractal and quasiperiodic photonic lattices. A common thread is engineering decoherence-free, geometry-controlled interactions between quantum emitters.

Before joining MPL, I studied physics in the *Elite Graduate Program* at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, with earlier work spanning quantum optics, computational neuroscience, and applied electromagnetics. I also spent several years as a software developer.
